The gap no one really prepares you for

Knowing how to teach is not the same as managing the reality of teaching.

Once you have your own class, the challenge is rarely one isolated lesson. It is keeping track of planning, behaviour, assessment, parent communication, differentiation, school expectations and everything else competing for your attention.

Coachivo helps you identify the real bottleneck, simplify it, and put a manageable system in place — without adding another complicated program to your workload.

A simple coaching process

Practical support built around what is happening in your classroom now.

01

Reflect

What is taking the most time, energy or headspace?

02

Diagnose

Work out the real problem underneath the overwhelm.

03

Simplify

Strip away unnecessary complexity and choose one practical next step.

04

Build

Create a simple system, routine or approach that fits your context.

05

Trial

Use it in the classroom and see what actually works.

06

Review

Keep what helps, adjust what does not, and move to the next priority.

Current mentoring offer

Three 1:1 mentoring sessions across the term.

Each session focuses on the problem creating the most workload or uncertainty at that point in the term. Between sessions, you can check in briefly when something comes up or you need help applying the system you are trialling.

The goal is not to give you more work. It is to make the work you already have easier to manage.

What you can expect

  • Three 1-hour individual sessions
  • Practical strategies tailored to your classroom
  • Simple templates or tools where they genuinely help
  • Light between-session support
  • No fixed program — support follows your most useful next step

About Coachivo

Built from classroom experience, not theory alone.

Coachivo was created by James Dabron, an experienced primary teacher with more than a decade in schools, experience mentoring pre-service teachers, supporting colleagues, leading curriculum and technology initiatives, and working across classroom, assessment and professional learning contexts.

Coachivo exists to bridge the gap between teacher education and confident day-to-day classroom practice — with support that is practical, responsive and grounded in the realities of teaching.
